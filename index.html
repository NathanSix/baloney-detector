What no detector can check for you

The detector only reads wording. A calm, well-written falsehood can pass every check here. Before you believe or share it, go to the original source and ask:

  • Has anyone independent confirmed this, or does it trace back to one source?
  • Who benefits if I believe it?
  • Are there other explanations that fit the facts just as well?
  • Does the full article actually say what the headline says?
